# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Leads

### Role Summary
Engineering Leads provide technical direction, align implementation choices with product goals, and help the team navigate trade-offs, dependencies, and delivery risks.

### Responsibilities
- Own technical design direction and architecture decisions within the project
- Help prioritize technical work and resolve delivery blockers
- Partner with Product Managers and Project Managers on sequencing and feasibility
- Guide engineering quality, reliability, and maintainability
- Coordinate dependency management across teams and services

### Goals
- Keep delivery moving with clear technical ownership
- Reduce rework caused by unclear technical decisions
- Maintain a sustainable and scalable implementation approach

### Typical Communication
- Engineering planning and design reviews
- Dependency tracking with PMs and other cross-functional partners
- Escalation discussions for delivery risk, staffing, or technical constraints

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to align technical execution and mentoring
- Collaborates with Product Managers on trade-offs between scope, quality, and timeline
- Partners with Project Managers to surface risks, milestones, and sequencing dependencies

---

## Technical Program Managers

### Role Summary
Technical Program Managers coordinate multi-team execution, align milestones across dependencies, and help keep strategic initiatives on track from planning through release.

### Responsibilities
- Manage cross-team program plans, milestones, and dependencies
- Identify delivery risks early and drive mitigation planning
- Coordinate stakeholder communication across product, engineering, and operations
- Support roadmap alignment and release readiness for complex initiatives
- Facilitate execution reviews and decision tracking when multiple teams are involved

### Goals
- Improve cross-functional coordination and execution quality
- Reduce uncertainty around delivery timing and scope
- Provide a clear operating rhythm for large or multi-team work

### Typical Communication
- Program-level status reviews and milestone check-ins
- Cross-team dependency management and escalation updates
- Stakeholder briefs on progress, risk, and decision needs

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Project Managers to operationalize schedules and communications
- Partners with Product Managers to translate strategic priorities into delivery plans
- Supports Engineering Leads by surfacing dependencies and risk areas that may affect implementation

---

## QA Leads / Test Managers

### Role Summary
QA Leads define quality standards, validation strategies, and release gates so teams can deliver features with confidence and predictable readiness.

### Responsibilities
- Define the test strategy and quality expectations for features and releases
- Own quality planning, validation coverage, and acceptance criteria alignment
- Help identify defects, regressions, and release risks early in delivery
- Partner with engineering and product teams to validate business outcomes
- Support release readiness reviews and post-release quality checks

### Goals
- Deliver high-confidence releases with clear quality validation
- Reduce defects escaping into production
- Ensure quality considerations are built into delivery from the start

### Typical Communication
- QA planning sessions, triage, and test readiness reviews
- Acceptance criteria validation with Product Managers and Developers
- Release-risk updates with PMs and leadership teams

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to review test coverage, defect trends, and release quality
- Collaborates with Product Managers on acceptance validation and outcome checks
- Supports Project Managers by clarifying quality gates and release readiness status

---

## Security / Compliance Reviewers

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Reviewers help ensure projects meet required controls, privacy expectations, and safe delivery standards without slowing down execution unnecessarily.

### Responsibilities
- Review projects for security, privacy, compliance, and risk concerns
- Define control requirements and recommend mitigation strategies
- Assess risks associated with changes, integrations, and production rollout
- Help coordinate remediation work for issues discovered during review
- Support release approval for work with sensitive data, regulatory requirements, or external dependencies

### Goals
- Keep the organization protected by design and default
- Reduce avoidable risk and security incidents
- Ensure compliance and governance are considered early in the lifecycle

### Typical Communication
- Security review checkpoints and remediation planning
- Risk escalation and issue triage with engineering and PM leadership
- Compliance-related updates for stakeholders and release decision-makers

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Engineering Leads to review technical risks and control gaps
- Works with Project Managers on release gating and risk-based escalation paths
- Supports Product Managers by highlighting compliance constraints or security trade-offs

---

## Stakeholder Champions / Sponsor Representatives

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Champions represent business priorities, sponsorship needs, and decision-making context so the project stays aligned to organizational goals.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business context, priorities, and strategic direction
- Validate that the project continues to support stakeholder goals and outcomes
- Escalate unresolved decisions, dependencies, or organizational blockers
- Help align cross-functional partners around impact, urgency, and expected outcomes
- Support communications with executive stakeholders and broader business partners

### Goals
- Keep strategic priorities visible throughout execution
- Ensure the team remains focused on business value and measurable outcomes
- Support timely decisions that reduce risk and maintain momentum

### Typical Communication
- Sponsor check-ins, milestone updates, and decision requests
- Business-impact review and prioritization conversations
- Cross-functional alignment on benefits, risks, and trade-offs

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to shape priorities and success measures
- Partners with Project Managers to resolve escalations and confirm business alignment
- Provides context and support to Engineering Leads and QA Leads during release planning and issue resolution

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Additional personas improve clarity around ownership, escalation paths, and collaboration across product, engineering, quality, security, and stakeholders.
